Privacy policy: Price Anatomy Uploader

Price Anatomy Uploader is an internal tool. Only the owner of the Price Anatomy YouTube channel uses it.

  • It requests the youtube.upload scope only. It uses that scope to upload the project's own videos, with their title, description and thumbnail, to the owner's channel.
  • It reads no data from other users' channels or accounts, and it does not access them.
  • It collects, sells and shares no personal data. Nothing is sent to any third party.
  • The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's computer. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
